How severity is scored

Each report gets a score from 0 to 100, calculated in your browser.

  • Issue type No signal 50, disconnects 35, slow 25, login 20
  • Weak signal +10 for every missing bar
  • Low speed +20 under 1 Mbps, +12 under 5, +5 under 15
  • Crowd +5 per confirmation, up to +20

70+ critical · 45+ high · 25+ medium · below 25 low

    About this project

    User: students and staff on campus who hit WiFi problems, and the IT support team who decides where to send technicians.

    Problem: complaints about WiFi arrive scattered over WhatsApp groups and emails, with no location, no speed data and lots of duplicates, so the worst dead zones are hard to spot.

    Runs in the browser

    • Form validation and the duplicate check
    • Severity scoring and live preview
    • Filtering, search, sorting and the heat map
    • Saving reports with localStorage

    Would move to a server later

    • Shared database of reports for everyone
    • Sign-in with university accounts
    • IT-only permission to resolve issues
    • Email alerts when a zone turns critical
